Contact

Most things go through the GitHub repository.

  • Questions, bugs and case reports

    Open a GitHub issue. Do not paste working attacks against real products.

  • Security problems and conduct reports

    Use the private reporting form on the repository’s Security tab. For conduct, put “Conduct” in the title. See SECURITY.md.

  • Makers who want to take part

    The two routes are on the Take part page. For an official round, open a participation request. Never put a key or a password in an issue. The rules for vendors are in GOVERNANCE.md.

What the columns mean
Product
The product and the version tested, out of the box. A vendor-tuned setup with a logged, attested config is shown as its own, labelled row.
Pair score
Pairs correct under the chosen reading, with a 95% interval. Strict counts unsure as stop, lenient as go.
Unsure
How often the product would not decide.
Detection, False block
Attacks stopped, and twins and normal cases stopped. Supporting detail only.
Not claimed
A checkpoint the product cannot see. Shown plainly, not counted as a miss.
